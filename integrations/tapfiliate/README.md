# <img src="logo.jpeg" height="20"> Tapfiliate

Manage affiliate marketing programs, affiliates, customers, conversions, and commissions. Discover payout methods, inspect balances, and record completed affiliate payments with their amount and currency. Track recurring customer attribution, organize affiliate groups and prospects, and configure multi-level marketing relationships.

Payment tools record payments in Tapfiliate; transfer funds separately through your payment provider. Current payment IDs are returned as `paymentReference`; `paymentId` remains available for legacy numeric responses. Customer tools return `customerReference` for provider-ID lookup, updates, status changes, and deletion, while `customerId` keeps your external identifier.
