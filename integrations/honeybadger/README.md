# <img src="logo.png" height="20"> Honeybadger

Search and manage application errors, notices, comments, projects, teams, and environments. Manage uptime checks and scheduled-job check-ins, view outages and deployment records, and query Insights. Report errors, events, deployment records, and check-in pings. US and EU accounts are supported. Discover account/project IDs and follow returned pagination links. Reporting may trigger project alerts, deployment reports may resolve errors, and team invitations send email.
