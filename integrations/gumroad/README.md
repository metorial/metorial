# <img src="logo.svg" height="20"> Gumroad

Manage digital and physical product sales on Gumroad. View and update product metadata, create and manage variant categories and options, and configure discount offer codes. Retrieve and filter sales data by date range for reporting. Manage subscribers and subscription lifecycles for recurring products. Refund sales, revoke or restore buyer access, and track payouts. Verify, enable, disable, and decrement license keys for purchase validation. Draft, schedule, and send audience emails to customers and followers. Read product reviews and access authenticated user profile information.
