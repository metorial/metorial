# Tidy

Manage properties, book and schedule cleaning and maintenance jobs, and coordinate service professionals through the TIDY property management platform. Create and manage property addresses, request and schedule jobs with preferred time windows, assign and manage service pros, track booking availability, manage to-do lists for job tasks, handle guest reservations for short-term rentals, and report and resolve property issues.

Send natural-language property requests with `message_tidy`, or use the structured
tools for specific operations. Discover property IDs with `list_addresses` and
check available service times and prices with `check_booking_availability` before
booking. Job time inputs use the local date and time at the property on a
30-minute interval.
