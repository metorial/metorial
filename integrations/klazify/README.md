# Klazify

Classify websites, domains, IP addresses, and emails into structured business data. Retrieve company profiles, revenue estimates, employee ranges, headquarters, technologies, social profiles, competitors, and domain registration data. Download brand logos, inspect IAB labels and category taxonomies, and discover domains by category, technology, headquarters country, company size, or traffic rank. Supported enrichment tools can refresh cached results on paid plans.
