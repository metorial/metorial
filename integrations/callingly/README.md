# <img src="logo.png" height="20"> Callingly

Automate outbound and inbound phone calls to connect sales teams with leads. Manage lead contact details, status, and call blocking. Initiate and schedule calls, retrieve call details including recordings and transcripts, and send personalized SMS messages. Discover and inspect teams, then configure call modes, whisper text, retry settings, and auto-SMS. Manage agent availability, scheduling, priorities, and team assignments. Discover client accounts and manage them as an agency partner.
