# <img src="logo.png" height="20"> Alpha Vantage

Retrieve equity prices, ticker quotes, company fundamentals, ETF profiles and holdings, earnings forecasts and transcripts, insider transactions, currency prices, commodities, economic series, technical indicators, market news, options, and global market status through 20 read-only tools. Use ticker search to discover supported instruments. Crypto prices use the requested market currency, and adjusted equity closes are exposed separately from raw OHLCV.

Data availability and freshness depend on your API plan. Quotes and top movers update at the end of the trading day by default; realtime or delayed US market data requires the corresponding premium entitlement. See the [API specification](docs/SPEC.md) and [official documentation](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/) for supported parameters and restrictions.
